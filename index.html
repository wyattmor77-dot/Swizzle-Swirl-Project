          ABOUT THE TECHNOLOGY

          AI Food Factory demonstrates how artificial intelligence could personalize customer experiences and control an automated food-production system.

          🧠 AI analyzes orders
          ↓
          📋 AI generates a recipe
          ↓
          🧩 AI determines the printing sequence
          ↓
          🖨 3D printer creates the food layer by layer
          ↓
          😋 Customer receives a personalized meal
          Important: this is a prototype simulation. The "AI" uses programmed logic (rules, comparisons and simple pattern counting) to show how a real AI system could work. It is not a trained machine-learning model, it needs no internet or API key, and it does not claim the machine can actually produce edible food. Real food 3D printers exist today but are still experimental.
          Order analysisCompares the food you built with the customer's ticket.
          Print planningTurns each ingredient into a layer and picks the right cartridge.
          PersonalizationSpots patterns in a returning customer's past orders.
          Built withHTML, CSS and JavaScript — every food is drawn with code, no images.
