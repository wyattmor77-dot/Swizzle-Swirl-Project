        SCHOOL PROJECT · HOW IT WORKS

        HUMANS CREATE. AI ASSISTS. AUTOMATION FINISHES.

        AI Food Factory imagines how artificial intelligence could assist people in a restaurant: the cook makes the food, an AI checks the work and plans the process, and an automated 3D food printer does the final finishing.

        1🧑 Customer places an order
        2👩‍🍳 Player prepares & cooks the food
        3🤖 AI checks the order for mistakes
        4🧩 AI plans the best synthesis sequence
        5🖨 3D food printer finishes the meal
        6😋 Customer receives personalized food
        7📊 System evaluates the result
        THIS PROTOTYPE SIMULATES AI-ASSISTED FOOD PRODUCTION.
        The "AI" in this game is programmed logic (rules, comparisons and simple pattern counting) that demonstrates what an AI assistant could do. It is not a trained machine-learning model, it needs no internet or API key, and the game does not represent technology that is available today — real food 3D printers are still experimental and cannot make meals like this.
        👩‍🍳 Human: cookingGrill, fry, bake, stretch, stack and pour. Your timing and choices matter.
        🤖 AI: assistingReads the ticket, recommends cook times, suggests stacking order and flags mistakes.
        🖨 Automation: finishingThe printer re-builds the food layer by layer from the AI's synthesis plan.
        💡 PersonalizationWith permission, the AI notices patterns in a returning customer's orders.
        🔍 Quality controlAn AI scan checks food, ingredients, cooking and presentation before printing.
        🛠 Built withHTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every food, person, machine and sound is generated by code.
        ETHICS

        RESPONSIBLE AI

        If a restaurant used AI like this for real, these are the rules it should follow — and how this game models them:

        🔒
        PRIVACYCustomer preferences should be collected responsibly and only with permission.In the game: insights appear only for customers who opted in to preference memory.
        ⚖️
        BIASAI recommendations should be tested for unfair assumptions about people.In the game: the AI only uses what someone ordered — never how they look.
        ©️
        COPYRIGHTAI-generated assets and content should respect creators and their work.In the game: every character, food and sound is original and made with code.
        👷
        WORKFORCE IMPACTAutomation may change restaurant jobs, so companies should plan retraining and keep humans involved.In the game: the human cooks; the machine assists and finishes.
        🧑‍🔧
        HUMAN OVERSIGHTAI should assist people rather than make every important decision automatically.In the game: the AI flags mistakes, but you decide whether to fix them.
